The package

Three layers. Each one has a job.

A new brand has three problems in a new market: nobody trusts it yet, nobody has seen it yet, and the people who saw it once have forgotten it by the time they shop. The package answers them in that order.

Credibility 2 × Biggest 190–300K followers

Nobody in Sweden has heard of you yet. When one of the country's biggest creators shows your product to her own audience, Swedish buyers see that someone they already trust has chosen you. That first signal is what the rest of the campaign builds on.

Reach 3 × Large 60–90K followers

Three large creators, each with a median of 38 000 views per video, carry the launch to the widest possible audience while it is new — so the brand is seen by as many Swedish buyers as possible in the same weeks.

Repeat exposure 5 × Mid-size 20–60K followers

People rarely buy a brand the first time they see it. Five mid-size creators, each with her own loyal audience, make sure the same buyers meet you again from someone else — that is what turns "never heard of it" into "I keep seeing it everywhere".

Plus 3 smaller creators at no cost — they always come with a campaign. Every creator makes one video on her own TikTok account, in Swedish.

FAQ

What brands usually ask

What does Swedish Market Entry cost?

SEK 54 054 excluding VAT for 10 creators with one video each: two of the biggest creators in our network, three large and five mid-size. That is the list price of SEK 59 400 minus our 9 % volume discount. 3 smaller creators come on top at no cost, so you get 13 videos.

Why these three kinds of creators?

Each layer does one job. The two biggest creators give a brand nobody in Sweden knows yet its credibility. The three large creators carry the launch to the widest audience. The five mid-size creators make sure the same buyers see you again, from someone else, in the same weeks.

Are the videos in Swedish?

Yes. Swedish creators make the videos on their own TikTok accounts, in Swedish, for their Swedish audience. That is the point: it is how a Swedish buyer meets your brand from someone they already follow.

Can we choose the creators?

Yes. We propose the ten creators with their numbers and why each fits your product, and you approve before anything is locked. You also approve every script and every finished video before it is published.

What if one of the biggest creators is booked?

Then a Top creator (90–150K followers) takes the place and you pay the difference less. With two Top creators the package is SEK 49 504.

Is the reach guaranteed?

We set a floor for the campaign, built on each creator's median over her last 20 videos — about 423K views for this package. If the campaign does not reach the floor, we add an extra video from one of our smaller creators at no cost. We can promise it because we measure every account every night.

Are the videos marked as advertising?

Yes. Swedish law requires advertising to be clearly marked from the start. Every creator gets a ready caption with the ad marking, TikTok's branded-content toggle is switched on, and we check both before a video counts as delivered.

Can we run the videos as ads?

Yes, with Spark Ads as an add-on. You buy the rights after publication, when you have seen what each video did organically, and run them from your own TikTok ad account. The media budget is yours; if you want us to set up and optimise the campaigns we charge 15 % of the media spend.
